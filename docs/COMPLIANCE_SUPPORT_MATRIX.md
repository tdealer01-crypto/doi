# DSG Spacetime — Compliance Support Matrix

DSG Spacetime provides technical controls and verifiable evidence that may support customer compliance work. This document does **not** claim automatic legal certification or blanket compliance.

| Framework / area | DSG Spacetime technical support | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| EU AI Act — risk management | Governed capability/Route boundaries, approvals, fail-closed execution, evidence | Customer remains responsible for legal classification, risk process, documentation and organizational controls |
| EU AI Act — logging / traceability | Durable execution evidence and verification model | Retention, required content and deployment-specific legal sufficiency remain customer responsibilities |
| EU AI Act — transparency / documentation | Public capability model, bounded execution context, evidence/proof outputs | System/provider-specific notices and required instructions must be completed for the actual use case |
| EU AI Act — human oversight | Routes may require approval/oversight before execution | Human-oversight design must match the actual AI system and risk class |
| EU AI Act — robustness / security | Fail-closed authorization, entitlement/deployment binding, bounded adapter execution, evidence | No claim that architecture alone satisfies every Article 15 obligation |
| GDPR | Customer-hosted architecture can keep systems/data in customer-controlled infrastructure and bound access through capabilities | Controller/processor roles, lawful basis, data-subject rights, transfers, retention and DPIA remain deployment-specific |
| Cyber Resilience Act | Source-free distribution, release integrity metadata, controlled security boundary and evidence can support product-security processes | Applicable obligations, vulnerability handling and conformity work require product/legal assessment |
| ISO/IEC 42001 | Governance/evidence model can support documented AI-management controls and audit evidence | Certification requires an organizational management system and independent certification process where sought |
| NIST AI RMF | Governed capability boundaries and evidence can support Govern/Map/Measure/Manage activities | DSG Spacetime is infrastructure support, not a substitute for the organization's full risk-management program |

## Positioning rule

Use wording such as:

> Designed to provide technical controls and verifiable evidence supporting AI governance and compliance work.

Do not state that DSG Spacetime automatically makes an organization or AI system fully compliant with a law, standard, or certification scheme.
